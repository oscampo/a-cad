# 1. Getting started

How to use the app: gestures with the pencil, finger and mouse, the parts of the screen, and how to select.

[← Basic concepts](conceptos.md) · [Index](README.md) · [2. Drawing →](02-dibujar.md)

---

## Apple Pencil or mouse

They draw and select.

## Finger

Pans the canvas and, with two fingers, zooms (pinch). With *Finger draws* ("View" panel) the finger draws too.

## Mouse

Wheel = zoom; middle button, right button or Space + drag = pan.

## Menu (☰)

Shows or hides the tools panel on the right. The first button on the bar opens the **element tree**.

- The bottom bar explains the active tool and shows the data of the selected element.
- Undo, redo, delete selection and fit view are on the right of the bar.
- Selecting: tap an element (tap several to add). The panel buttons are only enabled if the selection suits them.

## Finger magnifier

Touch and **hold** (a quarter of a second, without moving) and a circle appears with a **2x zoom** of what is under your finger, offset upward so your finger does not cover it; the crosshair in the center marks the exact point. Without lifting, slide your finger to fine-tune. It works with "Finger draws" on (any tool) and, with "Finger draws" off, in **Select**: on release, whatever is under the crosshair gets selected. If you move your finger before it appears, it is a normal pan. With "Finger draws" and Select, holding means aiming: on release, whatever is under the crosshair gets selected, without moving anything or making a box (the box is made by dragging right away from an empty spot).

## Cursor ring

The small circle that accompanies the pencil (over the screen) or the mouse also appears in the Select tool, at the cursor's real position.
