# 1. Getting started

How to use the app: gestures with the pencil, finger and mouse, the parts of the screen, and how to select.

[← Basic concepts](conceptos.md) · [Index](README.md) · [2. Drawing →](02-dibujar.md)

---

## Apple Pencil or mouse

They draw and select.

## Finger

The finger moves the canvas.

- **Pan**: one finger.
- **Zoom**: two fingers (pinch).
- **Drawing with the finger**: turn on ![Finger draws](../icons/hand.svg) **Finger draws** (in the [View](10-estilo-capas-y-vista.md#view) panel) and the finger draws too.

## Mouse

- **Zoom**: wheel.
- **Pan**: middle button, right button or Space + drag.

## Menu (☰)

![Menu](../icons/menu.svg) **Menu (☰)** shows or hides the tools panel on the right. The first button on the bar opens the ![Element tree](../icons/list-tree.svg) [**element tree**](10-estilo-capas-y-vista.md#element-tree).

- The bottom bar explains the active tool and shows the data of the selected element.
- Undo, redo, delete selection and fit view are on the right of the bar.
- Selecting: tap an element (tap several to add). The panel buttons are only enabled if the selection suits them.

## Finger magnifier

A circular magnifier with **2x zoom** lets you aim precisely with your finger.

### How to use it

1. Touch and **hold** (a quarter of a second, without moving). A circle appears with a **2x zoom** of what is under your finger, offset upward so your finger does not cover it. The crosshair in the center marks the exact point.
2. Without lifting, slide your finger to fine-tune.

If you move your finger before it appears, it is a normal pan.

### When it works

- With **Finger draws** on, it works with any tool.
- With **Finger draws** off, it works in [**Select**](03-seleccionar-y-editar.md#select-v): on release, whatever is under the crosshair gets selected.

With **Finger draws** and Select, holding means aiming: on release, whatever is under the crosshair gets selected, without moving anything or making a box (the box is made by dragging right away from an empty spot).

## Cursor ring

The small circle that accompanies the pencil (over the screen) or the mouse also appears in the ![Select](../icons/select.svg) **Select** tool, at the cursor's real position.
